ghealth

A personal command-line tool for reading my own Google Health data.

What it does

ghealth runs on my own computer and lets me query my health and fitness data from the Google Health API, such as steps, heart rate, sleep, and body measurements. I use it to build private charts and reports about my own sleep and activity.

Why it requests access to Google user data

To read this data, ghealth asks Google for read-only access through the following permissions:

  • Activity and fitness (read-only): steps, distance, exercise, active minutes.
  • Health metrics and measurements (read-only): heart rate, heart rate variability, weight, oxygen saturation.
  • Sleep (read-only): sleep sessions and sleep stages.

ghealth cannot write, change, or delete any health data. It is a personal project for my own use and is not offered as a service to other people.

Privacy

Data stays on my own computer and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. Read the full Privacy Policy.
