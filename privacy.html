Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

ghealth is a personal command-line tool that I built for my own use. This policy explains what data it accesses and what happens to that data.

Data accessed

With your permission, ghealth reads the following data from the Google Health API, read-only:

  • Activity and fitness data, such as steps, distance, exercise, and active minutes.
  • Health metrics and measurements, such as heart rate, heart rate variability, weight, and oxygen saturation.
  • Sleep data, such as sleep sessions and sleep stages.

ghealth does not request write access and cannot change or delete any data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show health and fitness information, charts, and reports to the person who authorized access, on their own computer.

Storage

ghealth runs entirely on the user's own computer. Access tokens and any data it downloads are stored only on that computer. There is no server, database, or cloud storage operated by ghealth, and no data is sent anywhere other than Google's own API.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or training machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ghealth's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the user's computer until the user deletes it. You can revoke ghealth's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all further access.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/abdulrahman1232/ghealth-app.
